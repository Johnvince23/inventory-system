About This System

The Inventory Management System is a simple yet powerful tool designed to help businesses and individuals track products, monitor stock levels, and manage inventory efficiently.

Features

  • Add new items with details and pricing
  • View complete inventory in one place
  • See real-time stock value and quantity totals
  • Low stock alerts to avoid running out of supplies
  • Fully responsive design — works on phone and desktop
